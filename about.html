About

Allie Sherman

I have been training my own dogs since 2008, and began taking on neighborhood clients and working with horses just a few short years later. My real passion took shape when I discovered dog sports and began following and attending seminars with some of the most talented trainers in the world.

In 2017 I traveled to Tryon International Equestrian Center, where I worked as a groom and trainer for a prominent competition program, preparing horses for Grand Prix events alongside Olympic teams and some of the best riders in the world. That experience confirmed something I already believed: the highest level of performance in any animal comes from trust, communication, and a relationship built on patience.

That same year I began my career as a veterinary technician, eventually specializing in emergency and exotic medicine. I still work as a relief veterinary technician today. That medical background shapes everything about how I read and work with animals. Understanding their physiology, their stress responses, and what they are actually telling you changes how you train.

Cognitive Canines is the combination of everything I have learned across all of it.

The Team

Meet the Belgians.

Three dogs. Three chapters. Each one a testament to what relationship-first training makes possible.

Chapter One

Oliver

Malinois Mix · The Beginning

Oliver was the first. A former cardiac alert service dog, he opened the door to what dogs are truly capable of when trust is the foundation. He is the reason this journey started.

Chapter Two

Kingston

Feb 2, 2016 — Mar 20, 2025

Kingston changed everything. A contracted working dog with the United States Military, he brought the world of sport and working dogs into focus. The standard we measure everything against. Forever in our hearts.

Chapter Three

Silas

Belgian Tervuren · The Culmination

Silas is the baby and the joy. A purebred Tervuren bred for sport, he represents the culmination of everything learned across Oliver and Kingston's chapters. Pure fun, pure elegance, pure drive.
