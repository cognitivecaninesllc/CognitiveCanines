Membership Programs

Find your tier.

Five levels of support, from self-paced foundations to elite sport and behavior work. All sessions are virtual. Dogs never interact with other dogs in any program.

Bronze

Foundation Access

Self-paced video content covering general obedience and puppy raising fundamentals. Learn at your own pace, on your own schedule.

+Pre-recorded training library
+General obedience curriculum
+Puppy raising guides
Monthly commitment

Silver

Live Group Access

Everything in Bronze plus live group video sessions where you can learn alongside other handlers and get real-time guidance from your trainer.

+All Bronze content
+Live group video sessions
+Real-time Q&A with your trainer
Monthly commitment

Gold

Private Coaching

Silver access plus private one-on-one video sessions with your trainer. Behavior foundations built from the ground up. Equipment is shipped based on your dog's individual needs and protocol.

+All Silver content
+Private 1-on-1 video sessions
+Behavior foundation curriculum
+Equipment shipped per your dog's protocol
2-month minimum

Platinum

Behavior Modification

Gold access plus advanced content targeting minor to moderate behavior modification. Your trainer will assess your dog's specific needs and ship the appropriate tools for their protocol — which may include a basket muzzle if required.

+All Gold content and sessions
+Advanced behavior modification content
+Protocol-specific equipment shipped
+Basket muzzle included if required
3-month minimum

Celestial

Elite Sport and Behavior

The highest level of coaching at Cognitive Canines. Platinum access plus elite guidance for serious behavior cases and sport dog training. Equipment is fully tailored to your dog — an e-collar will be included only if your trainer determines it is the right tool for your dog's protocol.

+All Platinum content and sessions
+Elite sport dog training
+High-level behavior modification
+E-collar included if required by protocol
4-month minimum
