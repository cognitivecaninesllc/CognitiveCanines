Five levels of support, from self-paced foundations to elite sport and behavior work. All sessions are virtual. Dogs never interact with other dogs in any program.

Bronze

Foundation Access

Self-paced video content covering general obedience and puppy raising fundamentals. Learn at your own pace, on your own schedule.

+Pre-recorded training library
+General obedience curriculum
+Puppy raising guides
+Community message board access
Monthly commitment

Silver

Live Group Access

Everything in Bronze plus live group video sessions and access to tier-specific message boards segmented by dog age and training focus.

+All Bronze content
+Live group video sessions
+Tier-specific message boards
+Age and focus segmented boards
Monthly commitment

Gold

Private Coaching

Silver access plus private one-on-one video sessions with your trainer. Behavior foundations built from the ground up, with equipment shipped to your door.

+All Silver content
+Private 1-on-1 video sessions
+Behavior foundation curriculum

Platinum

Behavior Modification

Gold access plus advanced content targeting minor to moderate behavior modification. A basket muzzle is shipped if needed for your dog's protocol.

+All Gold content and sessions
+Advanced behavior content
+Minor to moderate modification
+Basket muzzle shipped if needed
3-month minimum

Celestial

Elite Sport and Behavior

The highest level. Platinum access plus elite coaching for serious behavior cases and sport dog training at the highest level. E-collar shipped to you.

+All Platinum content and sessions
+Elite sport dog training
+High-level behavior work
+E-collar shipped to you
4-month minimum
